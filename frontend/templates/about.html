InvisiFace Technological Foundations

1. OpenCV Computer Vision

Face detection is performed via multi-scale Haar Cascades and OpenCV equalized grayscale matrix processing. Bounding box coordinates are extracted to isolate facial Regions of Interest (ROI) while preserving surrounding hair, clothing, background, and lighting.

2. Qiskit Quantum Feature Mapping

A 4-dimensional normalized feature vector (brightness, spatial contrast, area ratio, edge density) is encoded into rotation angles $\theta_i = \pi \times f_i$ across 4 qubits. Entangling $CZ$ quantum gates evaluate feature correlations via Qiskit Aer local simulation.

3. AES-256-GCM Cryptography

Processed images are encrypted into `.enc` binary packages using AES-256-GCM with a 12-byte secure random nonce. SHA-256 digests ensure end-to-end file integrity and protection against unauthorized tampering.
